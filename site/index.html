Welcome to Bookfania!

A reading companion for curious readers. Find a book, quiz yourself on it, keep your own library, and level up with friends.

Coming soon to app stores View on GitHub

Everything a reader needs

  • 📚 Find any book

    Search Google Books, with Open Library as a backup, and see page count, genre and estimated reading time at a glance.

  • 📝 AI quizzes

    Generate a quiz for the book you just read. Each book's title and author are checked against its catalog first.

  • 🔒 Private library

    Save books to your own library and leave star reviews of what you've read.

  • 👋 Friends & chat

    Add friends by friend code or username, chat with them, and see how they're reading.

  • 🎯 Quests & leagues

    Daily and monthly quests, plus weekly leagues where top readers move up.

  • 🦁 Animal avatars

    Pick an animal to represent you on your profile and the leaderboard.

Quiz yourself on what you read

  1. Pick a book from search or your library.
  2. Choose a difficulty level.
  3. Choose how many questions you want.

Answer multiple-choice questions, then see your results. Questions are generated by AI and checked for valid answers, and if one looks wrong you can report it right from the quiz.

Make reading a game

Daily quests

  • Earn 20 XP
  • Finish 2 quizzes
  • Get 10 correct answers

Weekly leagues

Ten leagues, from Scribbler to Legendary Librarian. Each week the top 5 readers move up and the bottom 3 move down.

Monthly badges

Complete 20 quests in a month to earn that month's badge.
